# Cute Dog Companion Mod

This mod adds a tameable, friendly dog to Hytale. Feed it a **bone** to tame it, then use a **whistle** to toggle sit/follow behavior.

## Files

- `mod.json`: Mod metadata and entry script.
- `entities/cute_dog.json`: Creature definition, spawn rules, and tame settings.
- `scripts/cute_dog.lua`: Tame, follow, and sit behavior.

## Behavior

- **Taming**: Feed a bone for a 45% chance to tame.
- **Commands**: Use a whistle to make the dog sit or follow.
- **Follow**: The dog follows its owner at a friendly distance.

## Notes

This is a lightweight scaffold designed to be adapted to the final Hytale modding API. Replace sound IDs, emotes, and item IDs with your actual content pipeline as needed.
